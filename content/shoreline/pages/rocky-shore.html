Tide Pool Field Guide

Rocky shores of the North Pacific

Who lives in a tide pool?

Twice a day the sea pulls back and leaves pools of water among the rocks. Look closely and they are crowded with life, sorted by how long each animal can last out of the sea.

Visiting a tide pool

Go at low tide, watch the waves and step only on bare rock. Turn stones back over the way you found them, and leave every animal in its pool.
