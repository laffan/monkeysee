The families who kept the light

Four families lived at Gull Point over seven decades, and their children grew up on the rock.

By Ruth Calder · November 24, 2026

Keeping a light was a family job. Wives and children trimmed wicks, polished brass and stood watch when the keeper was ill, though the logbooks rarely name them.

The archive has helped descendants find their own families in the pages. One reader found her grandmother's birth recorded between a gale warning and a delivery of coal.
