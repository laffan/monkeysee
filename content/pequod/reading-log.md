# Reading log

## Chapters 1–9

New Bedford, the Spouter-Inn and Father Mapple's sermon. Ishmael meets Queequeg and the two share a bed, then a friendship. The sermon on Jonah reads like a warning nobody aboard will hear.

## Chapters 16–22

Choosing the Pequod. The owners, Peleg and Bildad, haggle over Ishmael's share. The ship sets out on Christmas Day, and Ahab still hasn't been seen.

## Chapter 36

The quarter-deck. Ahab nails the doubloon to the mast and names the white whale as the purpose of the voyage. Only Starbuck objects.

The cetology chapters. Take notes on how the tone changes when the plot stops.
