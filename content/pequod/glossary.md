# Whaling glossary

- **Gam:** a social visit between two whaling ships that meet at sea.
- **Harpooneer:** the man who throws the harpoon from the bow of a whaleboat.
- **Mate:** an officer who commands one of the ship's whaleboats.
- **Try-works:** the brick furnace on deck where blubber is boiled down to oil.
- **Doubloon:** the gold coin Ahab nails to the mainmast as a reward.
