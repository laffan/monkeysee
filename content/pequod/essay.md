# The Crew as a Machine

Melville builds the Pequod like an engine. Ahab sets the course, the three mates turn his will into orders, and each mate is paired with a harpooneer who does the striking. Nobody on board is idle, and nobody is free.

What makes the arrangement frightening is how well it works. Starbuck sees the danger plainly and still carries out the orders, because the structure gives him no place to stand apart from it.

Ishmael is the exception. He has a seat in a whaleboat, but in the telling he floats above the machinery, free to digress for a chapter on rope or a chapter on whiteness.
